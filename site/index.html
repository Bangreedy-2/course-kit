Coursekit

Tell Claude what you want to learn. Get a whole course that checks your work.

Coursekit is a course player that lives in your own Claude account. It plans a week-by-week programme around your time and deadline, teaches it, runs your code, drills you from memory and keeps your place on every device.

Set it up in about two minutes

  1. Add the Coursekit plugin. In Claude, open Customize, then Plugins, choose Add marketplace and paste this:

    Bangreedy-2/course-kit

    Then pick Coursekit in the list and press Add. You do this once.

  2. Press the button. It opens Claude with the request already written. Press send, and approve when Claude asks to publish your player.

    Set up Coursekit →

    Claude publishes your private player, adds Python, checks it and gives you the link.

  3. Start learning. Open your player, press New course and say what you want: “Linear algebra for machine learning, 6 hours a week, exam on 12 March.”

Needs a paid Claude plan. Everything stays in your Claude account: no other sign-up, no keys.

What you get

  • A plan sized to youWeeks, projects with proof and timed checkpoints, built around your hours and your deadline.
  • Lessons that check youEvery lesson ends in practice the page marks, and recall cards that come back on a schedule.
  • Real code, really runPython and JavaScript run in the page. Answers are tested, not eyeballed.
  • A tutor that sees your placeAsk in the lesson you are on. The tutor can also reshape the course when you ask.
  • Your own notes become a courseAdd a PDF or notes and Claude teaches from them, in their order.
  • Same progress everywhereLaptop and phone show the same courses and the same streak.

Questions

Can I learn Elixir, C, Rust or use my GPU?

Yes. Those run on your own computer. On Mac and Windows, open your player in the Claude desktop app and press “Install as app” when a course asks. On Linux, or in Cowork and Claude Code, the plugin's runner does it and the course shows a Connect button. Code is sent only when you press Run.

Who can see my courses?

Only you. Your player is a private page in your account, and your courses and progress are stored with it.

Can I share a course with a friend?

Export it from your player as a file and send it. Your friend sets up their own Coursekit and imports the file. It becomes their copy.
